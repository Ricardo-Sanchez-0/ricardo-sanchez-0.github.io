Imaging & device development

PS-OCT-Guided Neurovascular Therapy Probe

A minimally invasive delivery system using polarization-sensitive optical coherence tomography (PS-OCT) to identify tissue-layer transitions near nerve interfaces and inform electrode placement for posterior tibial nerve stimulation.

PS-OCT23-gauge needle probeProbe fabricationBench test fixturesPorcine anatomy

My role & contributions

Co-inventor on the pending patent application “Apparatus for Applying Therapy to a Neurovascular Bundle,” published March 12, 2026.

  • Fabricated approximately 10 PS-OCT-compatible injectable probes and a motorized integration arm.
  • Designed and validated a needle probe and bench test fixtures for percutaneous electrode and drug delivery.
  • Developed test methods and authored protocols and reports supporting functional requirements and traceability.
  • Collected ex vivo datasets showing polarization-sensitive changes near the muscle–sciatic nerve interface.

Research & project context

The Neurosurgery Day presentation describes a 1.5-inch, 23-gauge optical-fiber needle probe mounted on a Siskiyou MX7600 micromanipulator. It presents initial tissue-layer observations in a porcine model and proposes further validation.
