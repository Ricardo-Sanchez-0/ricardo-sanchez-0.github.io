Peripheral nerve stimulation

Large-Animal Tibial Nerve Stimulation: Mapping On-Target vs Off-Target Effects

A porcine PTNS study examining how electrode placement and implantation depth affect selective tibial nerve activation. The updated resume documents 11 large-animal experiments with EMG and evoked compound action potential (ECAP) acquisition and analysis.

PTNSPorcine experimentsEMG / ECAPMicrodissectionHistology

My role & contributions

Lead experimentalist and protocol developer, working across Neuronoff, Case Western Reserve University, and Harvard/MGH.

  • Performed microdissections to map tibial anatomy and used histological imaging to characterize relevant structures.
  • Acquired and analyzed EMG and ECAP data to quantify on- and off-target stimulation as a function of implantation depth.
  • Connected stimulation geometry to nerve recruitment and electrode targeting strategies.
