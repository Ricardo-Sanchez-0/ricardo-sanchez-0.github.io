Anatomical modeling

Modeling and Image-Informed Device Interpretation

A 3D computational modeling pipeline integrating microCT, MUSE fluorescence imaging, and histological segmentation to build subject-specific anatomical models that guide electrode placement and device design iteration.

microCTMUSE fluorescence imagingHistological segmentation3D anatomical modeling

My role & contributions

Developed the anatomical modeling pipeline within the PS-OCT-guided Injectrode research program and coordinated across imaging, surgical, and signal analysis teams.

  • Integrated imaging-derived geometry with computational analysis of electrode placement and stimulation geometry.
  • Combined microCT, MUSE fluorescence imaging, and histological segmentation in subject-specific models.
  • Translated preclinical findings into device requirements, traceable design updates, and verification evidence.

Research & project context

This pipeline supports the tibial nerve stimulation and PS-OCT device programs. The linked resume describes the pipeline and lists the broader computational and CAD skills used across the research portfolio.
