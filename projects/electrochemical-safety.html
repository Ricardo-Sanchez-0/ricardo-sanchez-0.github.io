Electrode characterization

Electrochemical Safety Standards for Neuromodulation

Bench and in vivo characterization of implantable electrodes to assess charge injection limits and develop safety design guidelines. The work examines how electrode geometry, material, measurement instrumentation, and stimulation timing influence electrochemical behavior.

My role & contributions

Researcher in the Ludwig Lab and co-author of the DC offset measurement preprint; contributor to the 2023 NANS porous injectable electrode abstract.

  • Performed electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS), cyclic voltammetry (CV), and voltage transient (VT) testing using neural waveforms.
  • Compared geometric and electrochemical surface area effects on electrode behavior.
  • Developed a quantitative framework relating electrode geometry and pulse width to charge injection efficiency.
  • Evaluated electrode design data against safety thresholds to inform neuromodulation device design.

Research & project context

The supplied preprint, posted in December 2025, examines how measurement input impedance and stimulator leakage currents can bias DC offset and charge injection limit measurements. The NANS file is an editable abstract submission draft with working-title and submission guidance text.
