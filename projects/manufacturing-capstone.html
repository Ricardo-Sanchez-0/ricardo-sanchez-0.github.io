Medical device manufacturing · 2022

RELIANCE 4-FRONT Lead Manufacturing Process Redesign

A University of Puerto Rico–Mayagüez senior capstone sponsored by Boston Scientific in Dorado, Puerto Rico. The project redesigned the stake cable-to-fitting manufacturing process for the RELIANCE 4-FRONT lead.

Bench test fixture designFault detection sensorsVerification & validationISO 13485 manufacturingManufacturing transfer

My role & contributions

Led the manufacturing process redesign, collaborating with quality and manufacturing engineers on verification and manufacturing transfer.

  • Designed, prototyped, and deployed a motorized blade bench-test fixture to replace manual cutting operations.
  • Implemented in-line fault detection sensors to identify process deviations.
  • Authored design drawings, test protocols, verification reports, and work instructions.
  • Validated improvements through structured verification and validation, demonstrating approximately 50% less line dead time and an estimated $422,328 in annual cost savings.
