About Me

Building tools for translation

I am a PhD student in Biomedical Engineering at the University of Wisconsin-Madison in the Ludwig Lab. My work centers on peripheral nerve stimulation, electrochemical safety, PS-OCT-guided probe systems, and workflows that connect benchtop engineering to translational neuromodulation studies.

I am especially interested in how device geometry, anatomy, imaging, and stimulation safety interact when a prototype moves toward real clinical use.

Selected Projects

Featured case studies

Featured 01
Large-Animal Tibial Nerve Stimulation: Mapping On-Target vs Off-Target Effects

A 10-subject porcine PTNS study designed to quantify how stimulation distance and placement affect selective tibial activation.

  • Lead experimentalist and protocol developer across Neuronoff, Case Western Reserve University, and Harvard/MGH.
  • Performed microdissections to map tibial anatomy and collected EMG and neural activation data.
  • Linked stimulation geometry to on-target recruitment, off-target effects, and device targeting strategies.
Large animal PTNS EMG / neural data
Patent 02
PS-OCT-Guided Neurovascular Therapy Probe

Co-inventor on patent application 20260069758 for a PS-OCT-guided neurovascular therapy probe.

  • Named inventor on "Apparatus for Applying Therapy to a Neurovascular Bundle," published March 12, 2026.
  • Fabricated approximately 10 PS-OCT-compatible injectable probes and a motorized integration arm.
  • Collected ex vivo datasets with polarization-sensitive changes near the muscle-sciatic nerve interface.
Patent PS-OCT Probe fabrication
Safety 03
Electrochemical Safety Standards for Neuromodulation

Testing how electrode size, material, and geometry affect impedance behavior, voltage transients, and charge-injection claims.

  • Performed impedance spectroscopy, cyclic voltammetry, and voltage transient testing.
  • Compared geometric versus electrochemical surface area effects.
  • Contributed to a 2023 NANS abstract on a porous injectable electrode.
EIS / CV Injectrode Safety
Analysis 04
Modeling and Image-Informed Device Interpretation

Analysis pipelines combining microCT, histology, and image-derived structure to inform electrode placement and stimulation geometry.

  • Integrated imaging-derived geometry with computational reasoning.
  • Connected benchtop design questions with stimulation field assumptions.
  • Developed supporting infrastructure where anatomy, imaging, and device constraints meet.
microCT Modeling Device interpretation
